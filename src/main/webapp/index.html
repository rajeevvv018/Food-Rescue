How It Works

Simple. Transparent. Impactful.

FoodRescue connects the dots between surplus food and hungry communities in just four steps.

01
📋

List Surplus Food

Providers list their surplus food with quantity, expiry time, and pickup location.

02
✋

NGO Claims Food

Nearby NGOs discover available food and claim donations for their community.

03
🚴

Volunteer Picks Up

Volunteers accept pickup tasks and deliver rescued food to the NGO.

04
❤️

Food Reaches People

Good food reaches communities in need instead of going to waste.
